The Parking Problem

The next morning, Anvi was getting ready to go out when she heard her mother calling her.
"Anvi! Your scooter is blocking the gate. Move it a little."
"Coming, Mum!"
She picked up her keys and stepped outside.
As soon as she reached the gate, she stopped.
A black car was parked right in front of their driveway.
She stared at it for a few seconds.
"Seriously?"
She looked towards the neighbouring house. The front door was open, and Dev was
standing outside, talking to someone on his phone.
Anvi walked towards him.
"Excuse me!"
Dev turned around, still holding his phone.
He pointed towards himself as if asking whether she was talking to him.
"Yes, you. Can you move your car?"
Dev looked towards the car.

Why?"
