THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 12

Almost Friends

It was Sunday afternoon.
Anvi was sitting in her room, listening to music while arranging some old photographs on her
laptop.
Her phone buzzed.
Dev: Hey.
Anvi: What?
Dev: Are you busy?
Anvi: Depends.
Dev: On?
Anvi: Who's asking.
Dev: Very funny. Anyway, come outside.
Anvi: Why?
Dev: Just come.
Anvi stared at the message.
Anvi: You're not my boss.
Dev: Unfortunately.
She laughed and put her phone aside.
A few minutes later, she went downstairs.
Dev was standing outside his house with two cold drinks.
"Finally!"
Anvi looked at him.
"What happened?"

"Nothing. I was getting bored."
"And you called me?"
"Yeah. Is that a problem?"
"Usually, yes."
Dev handed her one of the drinks.
"Here."
She took it.
"What's this for?"
"Nothing. I bought two."
"Okay. Thanks."
They sat on the steps outside his house.
"So, what were you doing?" Dev asked.
"Looking at old pictures."
"Show me."
Anvi hesitated.
"Why?"
"Because I'm curious."
She opened her gallery and showed him a few pictures from school.
Dev started laughing.
"Wait. Is this you?"
Anvi looked at the screen.
"Yes. What's so funny?"
"Nothing. You look so different."
"Everyone looks different in school pictures."

He continued scrolling through the pictures she had selected.
"Who's this?"
"My best friend."
"Still friends?"
"Yeah. We've known each other for years."
"That's nice."
Anvi smiled.
"What about you? Show me your school pictures."
Dev opened his gallery and found a few old photographs.
Anvi looked at them.
Then she started laughing.
"Oh my God!"
Dev immediately took his phone back.
"What?"
"Your hairstyle!"
"What's wrong with it?"
"Nothing. It's just... interesting."
"Give me my phone."
She laughed even harder.
"Wait, let me see!"
"No."
For the next few minutes, they kept teasing each other about their old photographs.
After some time, Dev asked, "Do you miss school?"
Anvi thought for a moment.

"Sometimes. Everything was simpler back then."
"True. No deadlines, no college assignments, no responsibilities."
"And no annoying neighbours."
Dev looked at her.
"Still on that?"
"Always."
He laughed.
"Well, I don't miss school that much. I like where I am right now."
"Why?"
"Because I'm finally doing things I wanted to do."
Anvi nodded.
"That's good."
Dev looked at her.
"What about you? Are you doing what you always wanted?"
She took a sip of her drink.
"Not completely. But I'm figuring things out."
Dev smiled.
"Fair enough.
They sat quietly for a moment.
It wasn't an uncomfortable silence.
A little later, Anvi's younger brother came running outside.
"Di! Mum is calling you!"
"Okay, coming!"
She stood up and looked at Dev.

"I should go."
Dev nodded.
"See you."
She took two steps before turning back.
"Dev?"
"Yeah?"
"Thanks for the drink."
He smiled.
"Anytime."
That evening, Anvi was sitting with her best friend on a video call.
"So, what did you do today?" her friend asked.
"Nothing much."
"You're smiling. Something happened."
Anvi shook her head.
"Nothing happened. I just spent some time outside."
"With?"
Anvi hesitated.
"My neighbour."
Her friend immediately started laughing.
"ANVI!"
"Stop it!"
"I'm not saying anything!"
"You're literally laughing."
Her friend continued teasing her for another few minutes.

Anvi eventually changed the topic.
Meanwhile, Dev was in his room when Rohan called again.
"What's up, man?"
"Nothing. Just chilling."
"How's life next door?"
Dev laughed.
"Fine."
"Still fighting with that girl?"
"Not really."
"Oh? What happened?"
"Nothing. We just talk sometimes."
Rohan laughed.
"Interesting."
Dev shook his head.
"You're impossible."
That night, Anvi received another message.
Dev: Reached home?
Anvi: I was literally sitting outside my house

😭

Dev: Just checking.
Anvi: Okay, neighbour.
Dev: Good night, Anvi.
Anvi: Good night.
She placed her phone on the bedside table.
For the first time, talking to Dev didn't feel like an argument waiting to happen.
