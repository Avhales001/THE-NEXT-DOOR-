THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 14

A Little Closer

The next morning, Anvi was sitting at the dining table, having breakfast when her phone
buzzed.
It was Dev.
Dev: Good morning, neighbour.
Anvi: Good morning.
Dev: Something unusual happened today.
Anvi: What?
Dev: You replied without arguing.
Anvi: It's too early for your nonsense.
Dev: There she is

😂

Anvi smiled and put her phone aside.
A few minutes later, her mother walked into the dining room.
"Anvi, Dev's mother called. She invited us for lunch tomorrow."
Anvi nodded.
"Okay, Mum."
Her younger brother immediately joined the conversation.
"Can Dev bhaiya come to play today?"
Anvi looked at him.
"Is that the only thing you think about?"
"Yes."
She laughed.
That afternoon, Anvi was sitting in the garden, reading a book.
Dev came outside carrying two cups of coffee.

"Peacefully enjoying your Sunday?"
Anvi looked up.
"Trying to."
He handed her one of the cups.
"Here."
She took it.
"Thank you."
Dev sat on the nearby chair.
"You're welcome."
For a while, they both enjoyed their drinks.
Then Dev noticed the book in her hands.
"What are you reading?"
Anvi showed him the cover.
"Just a novel."
"Is it good?"
"Yeah. You should try reading something sometime."
Dev laughed.
"I read."
"WhatsApp messages don't count."
"Very funny."
Anvi smiled and returned to her book.
A little later, Dev's phone rang.
He checked the screen and answered.
"Hello, Rohan!"

Anvi continued reading while Dev talked to his friend.
"Yeah, I'm free this weekend."
He paused.
"Really? When?"
Another pause.
"Okay, I'll think about it."
He ended the call and put his phone away.
Anvi looked up.
"Everything okay?"
"Yeah. Rohan's planning a trip with some college friends."
"Oh, nice. Where?"
"Manali, probably. Nothing's confirmed yet."
Anvi nodded.
"You should go. It'll be fun."
Dev smiled.
"Maybe."
In the evening, Anvi was helping her mother prepare dinner when her younger brother came
running into the kitchen.
"Di! Dev bhaiya is calling you!"
Anvi wiped her hands.
"What happened?"
"I don't know. He's outside."
She went to the front door.
Dev was standing near the gate.
"Hey."

"Hey. What's up?"
"Actually, I wanted to ask something."
"What?"
"Would you like to come with us tomorrow? Mum is planning to visit a few shops before
lunch. She said you could join if you're free."
Anvi smiled.
"Sure. What time?"
"Around eleven."
"Okay."
"Great. I'll tell Mum."
He started walking away.
"Dev!"
He turned around.
"Yeah?"
"Don't forget your own plans this time."
He laughed.
"I won't."
The following morning, Anvi got ready and stepped outside at eleven.
Dev was waiting near the car.
"Ready?"
"Yeah."
His mother was already sitting in the passenger seat.
"Come, Anvi!"
Anvi greeted her and got into the back seat.

They spent the next couple of hours visiting shops, buying a few things and stopping for
lunch.
Dev's mother and Anvi were talking most of the time, while Dev occasionally joined their
conversations.
At one point, his mother went inside a shop and left the two of them waiting outside.
Dev looked at Anvi.
"Enjoying yourself?"
"Yeah. Your mum is really sweet."
"She likes you."
Anvi smiled.
"She's lovely."
Dev nodded.
After a brief pause, he said, "You know, when I first met you, I thought we'd never get along."
Anvi laughed.
"Same."
"And now?"
She looked at him.
"You're still irritating."
Dev smiled.
"Fair enough."
She laughed.
"But you're okay."
He looked at her for a moment.
"That's probably the nicest thing you've ever said to me."
"Don't make me take it back."

He laughed.
When they returned home, Dev's mother thanked Anvi for coming along.
Anvi went inside her house, changed her clothes and sat on her bed.
Her phone buzzed again.
Dev: Reached safely?
Anvi: Dev, we live next door

😭

Dev: Just asking.
Anvi: Yes, I'm home.
Dev: Good.
Anvi: Had fun today.
Dev: Me too.
Anvi: Tell aunty I said thank you.
Dev: Will do.
Anvi put her phone aside.
A few minutes later, another message appeared.
Dev: Also, I might be going to Manali next weekend.
Anvi: Oh, really? Have fun!
Dev: Thanks. I'll probably be gone for a few days.
Anvi: Okay, enjoy your trip.
Dev: Don't miss arguing with me too much.
Anvi: I'll try my best

😂

Dev sent a laughing emoji.
Anvi smiled and returned to her work.
That evening, Dev was sitting with his mother when she asked him something.

"Dev, when are you planning to go to Manali?"
"Probably Friday."
"Okay. Just let Anvi know if you need anything before leaving. She's been very helpful."
Dev smiled.
"Yeah, Mum. I know."
Meanwhile, Anvi was on a call with her best friend.
Her friend was telling her about something that had happened at college when Anvi
suddenly remembered Dev's trip.
"By the way, our neighbour is going to Manali next weekend."
Her friend immediately interrupted.
"Our neighbour? Since when did you start keeping track of his plans?"
Anvi laughed.
"I don't! He told me himself."
"Of course."
"Shut up and continue your story."
Her friend laughed and continued talking.
Anvi listened, occasionally smiling at her phone.
