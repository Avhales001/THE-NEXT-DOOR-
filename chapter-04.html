THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 04

The Unexpected Company

It had been almost two weeks since Dev moved into the neighbourhood.
Things had started becoming familiar. Dev had figured out where everything was, his mother
had already made friends with a few neighbours, and Anvi's younger brother had somehow
become his regular badminton partner.
Anvi, on the other hand, still found him irritating.
It was Sunday evening.
Anvi was sitting in the living room, helping her younger brother with his homework.
"Di, what's the answer to this?"
"Read the question properly first."
"I did!"
"Then why are you asking me?"

"Because I don't know the answer."
Anvi closed her eyes for a moment.
"God, give me patience."
Her mother walked into the room carrying a bowl of snacks.
"Anvi, can you take these next door? Dev's mother sent some food for us yesterday."
Anvi looked up.
"Why me?"
"Because you're sitting here doing nothing."
"I'm helping him!"
Her younger brother immediately interrupted.
"She's just scolding me."
Anvi gave him a warning look.
Her mother laughed.
"Just go, beta."
Anvi picked up the bowl and walked outside.
She rang the bell.
Dev opened the door.
He looked at the bowl in her hands.
"More sweets?"
"No. Your family keeps feeding us, so we're returning the favour."
Dev smiled.
"Come in. Mum's in the kitchen."
"No, it's fine. Just give this to her."
Before Dev could respond, his mother appeared from inside.

"Arre, Anvi! Come in, beta."
Anvi smiled politely.
"Namaste, aunty."
"Come, come. Why are you standing at the door?"
Anvi stepped inside.
Dev took the bowl from her and placed it on the table.
His mother started talking to Anvi about her studies, family and neighbourhood.
Meanwhile, Dev was sitting on the sofa, scrolling through his phone.
"Dev, why don't you make some tea for everyone?" his mother asked.
He looked up.
"Mum, I just made one for myself."
"So?"
He sighed and got up.
Anvi quietly smiled.
Dev noticed.
"Something funny?"
"Nothing."
"Good."
His mother looked between them.
"You two know each other already?"
Dev answered before Anvi could.
"Unfortunately."
Anvi turned towards him.
"Believe me, the feeling is mutual."

His mother laughed.
"Bas, bas. You both are neighbours. Stop fighting."
A little later, Anvi was about to leave when Dev's mother stopped her.
"Beta, wait. Take some snacks for your brother."
"Oh, no, aunty. It's okay."
"Arre, take them. I've made too many."
Anvi finally agreed.
Dev came from the kitchen carrying three cups of tea.
"Here."
Anvi took one.
"Thank you."
Dev sat down opposite her.
For a few minutes, they drank tea without saying much.
Then Dev noticed her looking at the family photographs on the wall.
"That's my school picture," he said, pointing towards one.
Anvi looked at it.
He had a much shorter haircut and was standing with a group of boys.
She smiled.
"That doesn't look like you."
"Why?"
"You look innocent."
Dev laughed.
"Was I supposed to look dangerous?"
"Honestly? You looked like someone who would actually listen to people."

"People change."
"Clearly."
Dev shook his head.
When Anvi finally returned home, her younger brother came running towards her.
"Di! Where were you?"
"Next door."
"Did you bring something?"
She handed him the snacks.
His face immediately lit up.
"Thank you!"
Anvi looked at him.
"At least someone in this house knows how to appreciate things."
Her brother was already busy eating.
"By the way, Dev bhaiya said he'll take me to the park tomorrow."
Anvi stopped.
"He what?"
"Tomorrow evening. He promised."
She sighed.
"Of course he did."
The following evening, Anvi was getting ready to go out when her mother called from
downstairs.
"Anvi! Take your brother with you to the park. Dev is waiting outside."
She came downstairs.
"Why me? He can go with Dev."
"Dev has some work. He asked if you could take him along."

Anvi picked up her keys.
"Perfect. Now I'm his babysitter too."
Her brother grabbed her hand.
"Come on, Di!"
Outside, Dev was standing near his car.
"Hey, Anvi."
She looked at him.
"Your brother is your responsibility."
Dev smiled.
"He's your brother too."
"Unfortunately."
Dev laughed.
"Have fun."
Anvi started walking towards the park with her brother.
After a few steps, she turned around.
"Dev!"
He looked up.
"What?"
"Next time, take him yourself."
"Sure, boss."
She shook her head and continued walking.
Dev watched them disappear around the corner before going back inside.
