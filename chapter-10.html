THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 10

The Rematch

It was Tuesday evening.
Anvi had just finished her college work when her younger brother came running into her
room.
"Di! Dev bhaiya is outside!"
Anvi looked up from her laptop.
"And?"
"He brought his badminton racket!"
She sighed.
"Okay, I'm coming."
She closed her laptop, tied her hair into a ponytail and went downstairs.
Dev was standing outside, spinning the racket in his hand.
"Finally. I thought you'd forgotten."

Anvi walked towards him.
"Some people have responsibilities, you know."
"Some people also make excuses."
She took the racket from him.
"Let's play."
Her younger brother immediately grabbed the shuttlecock.
"I'll keep score!"
Dev smiled.
"Perfect. And no cheating."
Anvi looked at him.
"Are you talking to me or yourself?"
"Both, actually."
They started playing.
The first few points were close, but Dev quickly took the lead.
"One point for me."
Anvi picked up the shuttlecock.
"Just play."
A few minutes later, she managed to score twice in a row.
Dev looked at her.
"Okay, where did that come from?"
"Talent."
"Interesting."
Her brother announced the score.
"Five-four! Di is winning!"

Anvi smiled proudly.
Dev shook his head.
"Don't celebrate too early."
They continued playing.
After a close final point, Anvi managed to win.
Her brother jumped excitedly.
"DI WON! DI WON!"
Anvi raised both hands.
"Finally!"
Dev stood there, looking at the racket.
"That was lucky."
Anvi laughed.
"Of course. Everything is luck when you lose."
Dev smiled.
"Fine. You won."
"Say it again. I want to record it."
He laughed.
"Don't push your luck."
After the game, the three sat on the nearby bench.
Anvi's brother went to buy ice cream from a vendor nearby.
Dev leaned back.
"Not bad, Anvi."
She looked at him.
"Thank you."

"You're welcome."
A brief silence followed.
Then Dev asked, "Do you usually play sports?"
"Sometimes. I used to play a lot when I was younger."
"What happened?"
"Studies, college, life. You know how it goes."
Dev nodded.
"Yeah. Happens."
She looked towards him.
"What about you? What do you do when you're not annoying people?"
He laughed.
"Quite a lot, actually."
"Like?"
"Football, music, movies. I like going out with friends."
Anvi nodded.
"Okay. At least you have some hobbies."
"Was that another compliment?"
"Don't get excited."
Around eight, Anvi's mother called her.
"Anvi, dinner is ready!"
She stood up.
"Coming, Mum!"
Dev picked up his racket.
"See you tomorrow?"

Anvi looked at him.
"Don't you have anything else to do?"
"Not tomorrow."
She laughed.
"Let's see."
Her brother came running back with his ice cream.
"Dev bhaiya, tomorrow also!"
Dev smiled.
"Your sister will decide."
Anvi looked at both of them.
"Why am I suddenly in charge of everyone's schedule?"
Dev laughed.
"Because you're the boss."
She shook her head and went inside.
Later that night, Dev was sitting in his room when his phone rang.
It was his old college friend, Rohan.
"Hello, idiot!"
Dev laughed.
"What's up?"
"Nothing. Just checking if you're alive.
How's the new place?"
"Good. Neighbours are nice."
"Any friends?"
"Yeah, a few."

Rohan paused.
"Any interesting people?"
Dev smiled slightly.
"There's this girl next door. We argue a lot."
Rohan burst out laughing.
"Already?"
"Shut up. She's just annoying."
"Sure, bro."
Dev changed the subject.
They continued talking for another twenty minutes before ending the call.
Dev put his phone aside and went back to watching his movie.
Meanwhile, Anvi was chatting with her best friend.
Best Friend: What are you doing?
Anvi: Nothing. Just resting.
Best Friend: Didn't you have that badminton match today?
Anvi: Yeah. I won.
Best Friend: Against whom?

😭

Anvi: Dev.
Best Friend: THE NEIGHBOUR?
Anvi: How many times do I have to tell you?

😭

Best Friend: Hahaha. Okay, okay. So, how is he?
Anvi: He's alright.
Best Friend: Only alright?
Anvi: Good night.

Her friend sent a laughing sticker.
Anvi smiled and put her phone away.
