THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 05

A Little Less Annoying

It was Monday morning.
Anvi was getting ready for college when she realised she couldn't find her earphones.
She checked her study table, her handbag and even the sofa downstairs.
"Where did I put them?"
Her mother called from the kitchen.
"Anvi, you'll be late!"
"Coming, Mum!"
She picked up her bag and rushed outside.
As she reached the gate, she noticed Dev standing near his car, talking on the phone.
She ignored him and started walking towards the main road.
"Anvi!"
She turned around.
"What?"
Dev held up a small white case.
"Looking for these?"
Her eyes widened.
"My earphones!"
He smiled.
"Found them in my car yesterday. You must have dropped them when I gave you that ride."
Anvi walked back and took them.
"Oh! Thank you. I was looking everywhere."
"You're welcome."
She checked the case.
"Wait, you didn't open it, did you?"

Dev stared at her.
"Why would I open your earphones?"
"Just asking."
"You're welcome again, by the way."
She smiled a little.
"Okay, thank you twice."
Dev laughed.
"Have a good day."
"You too."
Anvi started walking again.
Dev looked at her for a moment.
"That's new."
She turned around.
"What?"
"You actually said thank you without insulting me."
"Don't ruin the moment, Dev."
He laughed as she walked away.
That evening, Anvi returned from college feeling exhausted.
She dropped her bag on the sofa and went straight to the kitchen.
Her younger brother was already there, eating biscuits.
"Di, Dev bhaiya is coming for dinner."
Anvi looked at him.
"What?"
"Mum invited him and aunty."

"Since when?"
"I don't know."
She looked towards her mother.
"Mum, you didn't tell me."
Her mother smiled.
"I was going to. His mother invited us yesterday, so I invited them today."
Anvi opened the refrigerator.
"Okay."
Her brother grinned.
"Now don't fight with him."
She pointed a biscuit at him.
"Eat your biscuits and mind your business."
Around eight in the evening, the doorbell rang.
Anvi was helping her mother arrange the plates.
"Anvi, beta, open the door."
She wiped her hands and went to answer it.
Dev stood outside with his mother.
"Hi," he said.
"Hi."
His mother smiled.
"Hello, beta."
Anvi stepped aside.
"Please come in, aunty."
Dev entered behind her.

"Nice house," he said, looking around.
Anvi glanced at him.
"You've been here before."
"I know. I was just being polite."
"Try not to overdo it."
He smiled.
"Noted."
Dinner was going well.
Their mothers were talking about the neighbourhood, family and old memories.
Anvi's younger brother was busy telling Dev about his school.
"And then I scored two goals!"
Dev looked impressed.
"Really? That's great."
Anvi interrupted.
"One goal. The other one was an own goal."
Everyone laughed.
"DI!"
Dev started laughing too.
"Don't worry, champion. It happens."
Anvi smiled at her brother.
"See? At least someone understands."
Dev looked at her.
"You're enjoying this a little too much."
"Maybe."

After dinner, Dev helped Anvi's father carry a few chairs back into the living room.
Anvi noticed him helping without being asked.
She didn't say anything.
A few minutes later, Dev came into the kitchen to get some water.
Anvi was putting the leftover food away.
"Need help?" he asked.
She looked at him.
"No, it's okay."
"Sure?"
"Yeah."
He filled his glass..
"By the way, thanks for dinner."
Anvi smiled.
"You're welcome."
Dev leaned against the counter.
"So, how was college?"
"Long."
"That's it?"
"What else do you want? A full report?"
"Just asking."
She laughed quietly.
"Fine. It was okay. Had a lot of work."
"Same here. I've been unpacking and sorting things out all week."
Anvi looked at him.
What do you actually do, Dev?"

He took a sip of water.
"I'll tell you when you stop interviewing me."
She rolled her eyes.
"Forget it."
He laughed.
"Good night, Anvi."
"Good night."
Later that night, Anvi was lying in bed when her phone buzzed.
Best Friend: What's up?
Anvi: Nothing. Just had dinner.
Best Friend: Exciting life

😭

Anvi: Our new neighbours came over.
Best Friend: The annoying boy?
Anvi: Unfortunately.
Best Friend: Still annoying?
Anvi thought for a moment before replying.
Anvi: A little less.
Best Friend: OHHHH. Character development.
Anvi: Shut up

😭

She put her phone aside and smiled.
For once, she didn't have anything particularly bad to say about Dev.
