THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 13

Somewhere Between

It was Wednesday evening.
Anvi had been sitting in her room for almost an hour, trying to finish an assignment. She
finally closed her laptop and stretched her arms.
Her phone buzzed.
Dev: Hey, neighbour.
Anvi: What now?
Dev: Come outside.
Anvi: Why?
Dev: I need a favour.
Anvi: Ask your friends.
Dev: They're not here.
Anvi: That's unfortunate.
Dev: Anvi.
Anvi: Dev.
Dev: PLEASE.
Anvi laughed at the message.
Anvi: Fine. Two minutes.
When she stepped outside, Dev was standing near his car.
"What's the favour?" she asked.
"I need you to come with me."
"Where?"
"There's a shop nearby. I need to pick up something, but I don't know which one to get."
Anvi looked confused.
"What are you buying?"

"A gift."
"For whom?"
"My mother."
Anvi smiled.
"Oh, that's sweet."
Dev shrugged.
"It's her birthday tomorrow. I wanted to get her something."
"Okay. Let's go."
They started walking towards the market.
After visiting two shops, they finally found a small gift store.
Dev looked around, unsure what to choose.
Anvi picked up a beautiful handbag.
"What about this?"
Dev examined it.
"Looks nice."
"Then buy it."
"That's it? You're supposed to help me choose."
She laughed.
"Fine. What does she like?"
"Simple things. Nothing too fancy."
Anvi looked around and found a small pendant.
"This might be better."
Dev picked it up and smiled.
"Yeah. I think she'll like this."

"Then done."
He paid for it and thanked the shopkeeper.
Outside, they stopped at a small food stall.
"Want something?" Dev asked.
Anvi looked at the menu.
"Maybe momos."
"Good choice."
They ordered two plates and stood near the counter.
Dev looked at her.
"You're actually helpful sometimes."
Anvi laughed.
"You're welcome."
While they were eating, Dev's phone rang.
It was his mother.
"Hello, Mum."
"Where are you, beta?"
"Just outside. I'll be home soon."
"Okay. Don't forget to bring milk."
Dev looked at Anvi.
She was already laughing.
"Yes, Mum. I'll bring it."
He ended the call.
Anvi smiled.
"Milk?"

Dev sighed.
"Don't."
She laughed.
"Very responsible."
"At least someone has to be."
On their way back, they stopped at the grocery store.
Dev picked up the milk and a few other things.
Anvi was looking at the chocolates near the counter when Dev noticed.
"Want one?"
"No."
He picked up a chocolate anyway and placed it with the groceries.
Anvi looked at him.
"I said no."
"And I didn't ask."
She smiled.
"You're annoying."
"Learned from the best."
When they reached home, Dev handed Anvi the chocolate.
"Here."
She took it.
"Thanks."
"Thanks for helping me."
"No problem. Hope your mother likes the gift."
"I'm sure she will."

Anvi smiled.
"Good night, Dev."
"Good night, Anvi."
She went inside.
The next morning, Anvi was standing outside her house when Dev came out carrying a small
cake box.
"Hey!" she called.
Dev turned around.
"Hey."
"Happy birthday to aunty!"
He smiled.
"Thank you. I'll tell her."
"Did she like the gift?"
"She loved it."
Anvi smiled.
"Good!"
Dev looked at her.
"Actually, she asked about you."
"Me?"
"Yeah. She said she wants you to come over sometime."
Anvi laughed.
"Tell her I'll come."
"I will."
He started walking towards his car.

"Anvi?"
"Yeah?"
"Thanks again for yesterday."
She smiled.
"Anytime, neighbour."
That evening, Dev's family celebrated his mother's birthday.
Anvi and her family joined them for cake.
The house was filled with laughter, conversations and photographs.
At one point, Dev's mother called Anvi over.
"Come, beta. Let's take a picture."
Anvi stood beside her.
Dev joined them a moment later.
His mother looked at both of them and smiled.
"You two should stand together."
Anvi glanced at Dev.
"Why?"
"Because you both are neighbours!"
Dev laughed.
They stood beside each other while someone clicked the picture.
After the photographs, Anvi returned to her family.
Dev looked at the picture on his mother's phone.
"Nice photo," his mother said.
Dev smiled.
"Yeah, it is."
