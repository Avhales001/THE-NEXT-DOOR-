THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 16

Something Different

It was Thursday evening.
Anvi was sitting in the living room, watching television with her younger brother when the
doorbell rang.
"I'll get it!" her brother shouted.
He ran towards the door and opened it.
"DEV BHAIYA!"
Dev laughed and stepped inside.
"Hello, champion."
Anvi looked towards them.
"Why are you shouting? The entire neighbourhood can hear you."
Dev smiled.
"Good evening to you too."
"Good evening."
Her brother immediately grabbed Dev's hand.
"Come, show me what you brought!"
Dev looked at Anvi.
"Someone's been waiting for this since morning."
Anvi raised her eyebrows.
"I wasn't waiting."
"Of course not."

Dev took a small paper bag out of his backpack and handed it to her.
"Here. This is for you."
Anvi opened it.
Inside was a small handmade keychain with a little mountain design.
She looked at it and smiled.
"This is really cute."
"I saw it at a little shop and thought you'd like it."
Anvi turned it around in her hand.
"Thank you, Dev. I actually like it."
Dev smiled.
"Glad you do."
Her brother interrupted.
"Where's mine?"
Dev laughed and pulled out another small packet.
"Here, champion."
Her brother excitedly opened it and found a miniature car.
"THANK YOU!"
Anvi looked at Dev.
"You didn't have to bring anything."
"I know. I wanted to."
She smiled and placed the keychain on the table.
A little later, they were sitting outside.
Dev was telling them about his trip.
"One of my friends almost lost his phone while taking pictures," he said.

Anvi laughed.
"How?"
"He was so busy taking photographs that he forgot his phone was sitting on a rock."
"That's embarrassing."
"Very. We spent twenty minutes looking for it."
Anvi shook her head.
"Your friends sound just as irresponsible as you."
Dev looked at her.
"Why am I being attacked in every conversation?"
"Because you make it easy."
They both laughed.
After a while, Dev asked, "How was everything here?"
"Fine. College, assignments, the usual."
"Anything interesting happen?"
Anvi thought for a moment.
"Actually, my college is organising a cultural event next week."
"Really? Are you participating?"
"Not sure yet. My friends are trying to convince me."
"You should."
She looked at him.
"Why?"
"Because it'll be fun. You don't have to think so much about everything."
Anvi smiled.
"Look who's giving advice."

Dev laughed.
"Someone has to."
The following afternoon, Anvi received a call from her best friend.
"Anvi! Have you decided?"
"Decided what?"
"The cultural event! We're performing together. I've already submitted our names."
Anvi sat up.
"WHAT? You submitted our names without asking me?"
"Yes. You're welcome."
"You're impossible."
"Stop complaining. It'll be fun."
Anvi finally agreed.
After ending the call, she went downstairs to tell her mother.
Her mother was happy to hear about it.
"That's nice, beta. You should participate."
Anvi smiled.
That evening, Dev was sitting outside when Anvi came out carrying a notebook.
"Hey," he said.
"Hey."
She sat on the chair opposite him.
"Guess what?"
"What?"
"I'm participating in my college's cultural event."
Dev smiled.

"Finally!"
Anvi laughed.
"Why are you more excited than I am?"
"Because I told you to participate."
"That doesn't mean you get the credit."
"Fine. I'll accept a thank-you."
"Keep dreaming."
He laughed.
They talked for a while before Anvi's mother called her inside.
She stood up.
"See you later."
"Good luck with the preparations."
"Thanks."
Over the next few days, Anvi became busy with rehearsals and college work.
Dev also had his own commitments, so they didn't get much time to sit together.
Still, they occasionally exchanged messages.
One evening, Anvi was returning home after rehearsal when she noticed Dev waiting near
the gate.
"Hey! What are you doing here?"
"Waiting for you."
She looked surprised.
"Why?"
He held out a small packet.
"Your mother said you hadn't eaten anything since afternoon. I got you something."

Anvi took the packet.
"You went out just to get this?"
"I was going out anyway."
She smiled.
"Thank you."
"You're welcome. How was rehearsal?"
"Tiring. We had to repeat the same thing about twenty times."
Dev laughed.
"Sounds exhausting."
"It was."
He looked at her.
"Don't worry. You'll do great."
Anvi smiled.
"Let's hope so."
That night, Anvi was sitting on her bed, eating the food Dev had brought.
Her phone buzzed.
Dev: Ate something?
Anvi: Yes, thanks to you.
Dev: Good.
Anvi: You're becoming unnecessarily caring these days.
Dev: Should I stop?
Anvi looked at the message for a moment.
Anvi: No. It's okay.
Dev: Good to know

😂

Anvi: Good night, Dev.
Dev: Good night, Anvi. And good luck for your event.
She smiled and put her phone aside.
