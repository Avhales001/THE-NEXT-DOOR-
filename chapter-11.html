THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 11

A Different Side

It was Thursday afternoon.
Anvi had just returned from college. She was walking towards her house when she noticed
Dev sitting outside with his laptop.
He seemed busy, typing something while occasionally checking his phone.
Anvi slowed down as she passed him.
"Hey."
Dev looked up.
"Hey. Back from college?"
"Yeah. What are you doing?"
"Work."
She looked at his laptop.
"Actual work? I'm impressed."
Dev laughed.
"Very funny."
Anvi smiled and started walking again.
"Wait."
She turned around.
"Yeah?"
"Do you have a charger?"
"For what?"
"My laptop. I think I left mine somewhere."
Anvi thought for a moment.

"I might have one. Wait."
She went inside and returned with a charger.
"Try this."
Dev checked it.
"Perfect. Thanks."
"No problem."
She was about to leave when Dev asked, "Are you free?"
"Why?"
"Can you sit here for five minutes? I need to finish something, and I'm getting bored."
Anvi raised an eyebrow.
"So you want me to entertain you?"
"Exactly."
She laughed.
"You're unbelievable."
But she sat down on the chair opposite him.
For the next few minutes, Dev continued working while Anvi scrolled through her phone.
Occasionally, she glanced at his screen.
"What are you working on?" she asked.
Dev turned the laptop slightly.
"Just editing something for a project."
Anvi looked at the screen.
"Oh, you do editing?"
"Yeah. Sometimes."
"That's actually interesting."

Dev smiled.
"Finally, something about me that you like."
"Don't ruin it."
He laughed and went back to work.
A few minutes later, he closed the laptop.
"Done."
Anvi put her phone away.
"Congratulations."
"Thank you for your valuable support."
"Anytime."
They both laughed.
Just then, Dev's phone rang.
He checked the screen.
His smile faded a little.
He answered the call.
"Hello, Dad."
Anvi wasn't trying to listen, but she noticed his expression changing.
"Yeah, I know."
A pause.
"I'll take care of it. Don't worry."
Another pause.
"Okay, Dad. I'll call you later."
He ended the call and put his phone down.
Anvi looked at him.

"Everything okay?"
Dev nodded.
"Yeah. Just some family stuff."
She didn't ask anything further.
"Okay."
There was a small silence.
Then Dev looked at her.
"Thanks for asking."
Anvi smiled.
"You're welcome."
After a while, Anvi stood up.
"I should go. I have some work."
"Sure."
She picked up the empty glass from the table.
"By the way, you should get your own charger."
Dev laughed.
"I'll try."
"Don't try. Do it."
"Yes, ma'am."
Anvi walked towards her gate.
"See you later."
"See you."
That evening, Anvi was sitting with her mother in the kitchen.
Her mother was preparing dinner while Anvi helped cut vegetables.

"Anvi, did you meet Dev today?"
She looked up.
"Yeah. He was sitting outside."
"He's a nice boy."
Anvi smiled.
"Yeah, he is."
Her mother looked at her.
"You both seem to be getting along now."
Anvi shrugged.
"He's not as irritating as I thought."
Her mother laughed.
"That's progress."
Anvi smiled and continued cutting vegetables.
Later that night, Dev sent her a message.
Dev: Hey.
Anvi: What?
Dev: Just wanted to say thanks for the charger.
Anvi: You're welcome.
Dev: I'll return it tomorrow.
Anvi: No rush.
Dev: Also, you were good company today.
Anvi read the message and smiled.
Anvi: Don't get used to it.
Dev: Too late

😂

She put her phone aside and continued watching her show.
