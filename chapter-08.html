THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 08

A Day Out

It was Sunday morning.
Anvi was sitting in her room, trying to finish an assignment when her phone started ringing.
It was her best friend.
"Hello?"
"Anvi! Are you free today?"
"Depends. Why?"
"Let's go out. I'm bored."
Anvi looked at her unfinished assignment.
"I have some work."
"Please! We'll come back by evening."
Anvi thought for a moment.
"Okay, fine. Give me an hour."
"That's my girl!"
The call ended.
Anvi got up and started getting ready.
Around an hour later, Anvi came downstairs with her handbag.
Her mother looked at her.
"Going somewhere?"
"Yeah, Mum. Going out with my friend."
"Okay. Come back before dinner."
"Sure."
As she opened the front door, she noticed Dev standing outside his house, trying to fix
something on his bicycle.
He looked up.

"Going somewhere?"
Anvi stopped.
"Why? Do I need permission?"
Dev laughed.
"Just asking."
"Then yes, I'm going somewhere."
"Enjoy."
She started walking towards the main road.
"Anvi!"
She turned around.
"What now?"
"Your bag is open."
She looked down and quickly closed it.
Dev smiled.
"You're welcome."
She shook her head.
"Thanks."
A little later, Anvi met her best friend at a café.
They ordered their drinks and found a table near the window.
"So," her friend started, "how's the new neighbour?"
Anvi immediately looked at her.
"Can we please talk about something else?"
Her friend laughed.
"Why? I'm just asking."

"He's fine. Happy?"
"Very."
They spent the next few hours talking about college, friends, family and everything else.
After lunch, they decided to visit a nearby shopping street.
Anvi was looking through a clothing store when her phone rang.
It was her mother.
"Hello, Mum?"
"Beta, can you bring some bread and milk when you come back?"
"Okay, Mum."
She ended the call and returned to browsing.
By the time Anvi returned home, it was almost seven.
She stopped at a grocery shop, bought the things her mother had asked for and started
walking towards her house.
As she reached the neighbourhood, she noticed Dev sitting outside with a cup of coffee.
He looked up.
"Back?"
Anvi nodded.
"Yeah."
"Good day?"
"Pretty good."
Dev looked at the shopping bags in her hand.
"Need help?"
"No, it's fine."
He stood up anyway.

"Give me one."
"Dev, I can carry them."
"I know. But I can help too."
She looked at him for a moment before handing over the heavier bag.
"Fine."
They walked towards her gate.
"So, where did you go?" he asked.
"Shopping and lunch with a friend."
"Nice."
"What about you?"
"Nothing special. Went out with a friend in the afternoon."
They reached her gate.
Dev handed her the bag.
"Here."
"Thanks."
"You're welcome."
Anvi started walking inside.
"Hey, Anvi."
She turned around.
"Yeah?"
"Next Sunday, we're playing badminton.
No excuses."
She smiled.
"We'll see."
"That's not a yes."

"That's all you're getting."
Dev laughed.
"Fine. I'll take it."
That night, Anvi was sitting with her family when her younger brother suddenly announced:
"Tomorrow, Dev bhaiya is coming to help me with my project!"
Anvi looked up.
"Since when?"
"I asked him."
Her mother smiled.
"That's nice. Dev is such a helpful boy."
Anvi nodded.
"Yeah, he is."
Her brother looked at her.
"See? Even you agree!"
Anvi picked up a cushion and threw it at him.
"Finish your dinner."
Everyone laughed.
