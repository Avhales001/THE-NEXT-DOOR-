THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 01

The New Neighbour

It was a Sunday morning.
Anvi was sitting on the balcony, enjoying her morning tea while scrolling through her phone.
It was one of those peaceful mornings when nothing interesting was supposed to happen.
Until a moving truck stopped outside the house next door.
She barely paid attention at first. The house had been empty for quite some time, and seeing
someone finally move in wasn't exactly exciting.
A few minutes later, she noticed a boy carrying a cardboard box towards the entrance.
He looked around, spoke briefly to the movers and disappeared inside.
"Finally, someone bought that house," Anvi murmured before returning to her phone.
She was about to take another sip of tea when a loud noise came from next door.
Something had fallen.
She looked towards the house again.
A boy came outside, looking slightly annoyed.
Anvi watched for a moment before quietly laughing to herself.
First day, and he's already creating a mess.
little later, her mother called her from inside.
"Anvi! Can you go next door and give them this box of sweets? They've just moved in."
Anvi looked at the box.
"Abhi?"
"Yes, abhi. And be polite."
She sighed, picked up the sweets and walked towards the neighbouring house.
She rang the bell.
After a few seconds, the door opened.
It was the same boy from earlier.

"Yes?"
Anvi held out the box.
"Hi. We're your neighbours. My mother sent these for you."
He looked at the box, then at her.
"Oh. Thank you."
"You're welcome."
There was a brief silence.
Then he asked, "You live next door?"
Anvi looked at him.
"No, I came from another city just to deliver sweets."
He raised an eyebrow.
"Okay. No need to be sarcastic."
"Then don't ask obvious questions."
He looked at her for a second, almost as if he was trying to decide whether to respond.
Finally, he took the box.
"Anyway, thanks."
Anvi gave him a small nod and turned to leave.
"By the way, I'm Dev."
She stopped for a moment.
"Anvi."
Nice to meet you."
"Let's see about that."
And with those words, she walked away.
Dev stood at the door for another second before shaking his head.

"What a neighbour..."
Meanwhile, Anvi returned home and placed her empty hands on the kitchen counter.
Her mother asked, "De diye sweets?"
"Haan, de diye."
"What's the boy's name?"
Anvi picked up her phone.
"Dev. And I think he's going to be a headache."
Next door, Dev was unpacking his things.
