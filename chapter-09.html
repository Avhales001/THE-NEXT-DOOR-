THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 09

The Project Partner

It was Monday afternoon.
Anvi had just returned from college when she found her younger brother sitting at the dining
table with his school project spread all over it.
There were coloured papers, glue, scissors and a few drawings scattered around.
She stopped near the table.
"What happened here?"

Her brother looked up.
"My project."
Anvi stared at the mess.
"Looks more like a disaster."
"Di!"
She laughed and went upstairs to change.
A little later, the doorbell rang.
Her brother immediately ran towards it.
"DEV BHAIYA!"
Dev entered carrying a small bag.
"Hey, champion. Ready for the project?"
"Yes!"
Anvi came downstairs after hearing his voice.
She looked at Dev.
"Oh, you're here."
Dev smiled.
"Nice welcome."
"Don't expect too much."
He laughed and sat beside her brother.
For the next hour, Dev helped Anvi's brother with his school project.
Anvi was sitting on the sofa, working on her laptop.
Occasionally, she looked towards them.
"Dev bhaiya, this colour or that one?"
"That one looks better."

"Should I write this here?"
"Yeah, that's fine."
After a while, Dev looked at the clock.
"Okay, we should finish this part today."
Anvi looked up.
"You're taking this more seriously than he is."
Dev turned towards her.
"Someone has to."
Her brother laughed.
"Di, see? Dev bhaiya is better than you."
Anvi closed her laptop.
"Excuse me?"
Dev tried to hide his smile.
"I didn't say anything."
"You don't have to."
A few minutes later, Anvi's brother went upstairs to get another notebook.
Dev started collecting the paper scraps from the table.
Anvi noticed and got up.
"Leave it. I'll clean this."
"It's okay. I'm already doing it."
She picked up the scissors.
"Still, you didn't have to come and help him."
Dev shrugged.
"He asked. I was free."

Anvi smiled.
"You're actually good with kids."
Dev looked at her.
"Was that a compliment?"
"Don't make it a big deal."
"Too late. I'm going to remember this."
She laughed.
"You're impossible."
Around six, Dev finished helping with the project.
Her brother was extremely happy with the result.
"Thank you, Dev bhaiya!"
"You're welcome. Just make sure you finish the written part yourself."
"Okay!"
Dev picked up his bag and stood up.
Anvi walked him to the door.
"Thanks for helping him."
"No problem."
"He listens to you more than he listens to me."
Dev smiled.
"Maybe because I don't give him homework."
Anvi laughed.
"Good point."
He stepped outside.
"See you later."

"Bye."
That evening, Anvi was sitting on the balcony when she noticed Dev returning from
somewhere.
He looked up and spotted her.
"Hey!"
"Hey."
"Done with your work?"
"Almost."
He nodded.
"Good luck with it."
"Thanks."
Dev started walking towards his house, then turned back.
"Anvi?"
"Yeah?"
"Do you want to play badminton tomorrow?"
She smiled.
"Don't you ever get tired of asking?"
"Nope."
"Fine. Tomorrow."
Dev raised his eyebrows.
"Really?"
"Yes. Now go."
He smiled.
"See you tomorrow, then."
Anvi watched him enter his house before returning to her laptop.

A little later, her phone buzzed.
Dev: Don't forget tomorrow.
Anvi: I won't.
Dev: Good. I need a rematch.
Anvi: You won last time.
Dev: Exactly. I want to make sure it wasn't luck.
Anvi: Keep dreaming

😂

Dev: See you tomorrow, loser.
Anvi: GOOD NIGHT.
Dev sent a laughing emoji.
Anvi put her phone aside and continued working.
