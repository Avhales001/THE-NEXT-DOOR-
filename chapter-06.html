THE NEXT DOOR · CHAPTER 06

The Rainy Evening

It was a Friday evening.

Anvi had just returned from college when she noticed the weather changing. The sky was
covered with dark clouds, and a cool breeze was blowing through the streets.
She left her bag on the sofa and went to the balcony.
"Looks like it's going to rain," she murmured.
Her mother called from downstairs.
"Anvi! Bring the clothes inside before it starts!"
"Coming, Mum!"
She stepped onto the balcony and started collecting the clothes.
Just as she picked up the last few hangers, the first drops of rain began falling.
"Great timing."
She quickly gathered everything and went inside.
A few minutes later, it started raining heavily.
Anvi made herself a cup of tea and settled on the sofa with her phone.
She was halfway through a video when the electricity went off.
"Seriously?"
Her younger brother shouted from his room.
"DI! LIGHTS!"
"I KNOW!"
She switched on her phone's flashlight and walked towards the kitchen.
Outside, the rain continued.
Next door, Dev was standing near his window, watching the rain.
His phone battery was almost dead, and the power cut had ruined his plans to watch a
movie.
He went downstairs to look for a candle.
As he stepped outside to check the inverter, he noticed Anvi standing near her gate.

She was trying to move a few flowerpots away from the rain.
Dev walked towards her.
"Need help?"
Anvi looked up.
"No, I'm fine."
One of the pots slipped slightly.
Dev quickly caught it.
"Yeah, I can see that."
Anvi gave him a look.
"Can you just hold this?"
He took the pot from her.
Together, they moved the remaining pots under the covered area.
"Done," Dev said.
"Thanks."
"You're welcome."
They stood near the gate for a moment, watching the rain.
Dev looked towards her.
"So, do you always have something to do, or is it just today?"
Anvi smiled.
"Some of us don't spend the entire day doing nothing."
"That's a very personal attack."
"Good. You understood it."
He laughed.
A few minutes later, Anvi went inside and returned with two cups of tea.

Dev looked surprised.
"What's this?"
"Tea. Usually people drink it."
"I know what tea is."
"Then why are you asking?"
He took the cup.
"Thank you."
They stood under the balcony roof, enjoying the rain.
Dev took a sip.
"Not bad."
Anvi looked at him.
"Not bad?"
"Yeah. Could use a little more sugar."
"Next time, make it yourself."
Dev smiled.
"Next time?"
Anvi realised what she'd said.
"I mean, if there is a next time."
"Sure."
For a while, neither said anything.
Then Dev asked, "Do you like rain?"
Anvi looked towards the road.
"Yeah. Especially when I don't have to go anywhere."
"Same."

She smiled.
"At least we agree on something."
"Don't get used to it."
Anvi laughed.
Just then, Dev's phone rang.
He checked the screen.
"Mum."
He answered the call.
"Hello, Mum?"
A few seconds passed.
"Yeah, I'm outside."
He listened and nodded.
"Okay, I'll come."
He ended the call.
"Have to go. Mum needs something."
Anvi nodded.
"Okay."
Dev placed his empty cup on the nearby table.
"Thanks for the tea."
"You're welcome."
He started walking towards his house.
"Anvi?"
She looked at him.
"Yeah?"

"Your tea was actually good."
She smiled.
"Go before I change my mind about being nice to you."
Dev laughed and went inside.
Later that night, the electricity returned.
Anvi was sitting in her room when her phone buzzed.
Dev: Hey.
She looked at the screen, confused.
Anvi: Hey?
Dev: Thanks again for the tea.
Anvi: You already said that.
Dev: Just making sure you know I appreciated it.
Anvi: Okay

😂

Dev: Also, I think you owe me another game of badminton.
Anvi: Dream on.
Dev: Scared?
Anvi: Good night, Dev.
Dev: Good night, neighbour.
Anvi put her phone aside and went back to her work.
A few seconds later, she picked it up again to check whether he'd sent another message.
Nothing.
She shook her head and smiled before returning to her notebook.
